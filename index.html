a quiet message
01 — what i've been feeling

We used to be so close, and I felt so full of love. When you used to say things like “tmi mero mutu hau ni,” “you’re my world,” “my Manti”… those little things made me feel so loved. But lately, things feel different between us, and honestly, I really miss hearing those things from you.

Maybe some of the things I’ve done made you lose interest or made your feelings change, and that’s something I carry a lot of guilt about. I know I’ve made mistakes, and I genuinely regret them.

I know you love me, and I’m not trying to question that. But sometimes, hearing you express it helps calm my mind. I could reassure you a thousand times when you were scared or couldn’t sleep because I wanted you to feel safe and loved. Lately, I’ve been missing that same feeling from you. Even getting an “I love you” without me saying it first feels rare now.

02 — about our memories

And about the things we did on our dates… knowing that you regret some of those moments honestly hurts me, not because I’m angry at you, but because I never wanted you to look back at the time we spent together and feel regret.

I’m really sorry if anything I did made you feel that way. And now, knowing that you regret those moments, I find myself regretting them too—not because I want to dismiss how you feel, but because I never wanted something that meant so much to me to become something that hurts you.

03 — please remember this

But I also hope you remember the good things we had. The little conversations, the laughs, the way we looked at each other, the time we spent together, the stupid things we did, and all those small moments that probably didn't seem important at the time.

For me, they were real.

Every bit of happiness I felt with you was real, and the love I had for you during those moments was real too.

04 — i'm sorry

I don't want you to remember our relationship only through the things that went wrong or the things you regret. I hope you can still remember that there were moments when we genuinely made each other happy, when we felt safe with each other, when we couldn't wait to see each other, and when even doing something simple together felt special.

I know I can't change the past, and I don't want to force you to feel differently about anything. I just want you to know that I cherish the time we spent together, and I’m sorry for anything that made those memories painful for you.

If I could go back, I would be more careful with your feelings, because the last thing I ever wanted was for the person I love to look back at our memories and wish they never happened.

05 — the truth

I'm not saying all of this to make you feel guilty or to ask you to prove your love to me. I just wanted you to understand what has been going on in my head and why I've been feeling this way.

Maybe I'm overthinking, maybe I'm seeking too much reassurance, or maybe something really has changed. I honestly don't know.

I just want to understand you instead of making assumptions.

06 — what i still know

I still love you.

I love the person you are, I love the memories we made, and I still care about us deeply.

I just miss feeling that closeness we used to have, and sometimes I wish I could feel a little bit of that again.

you don't have to answer everything right now.

just be honest with me.

I would rather hear the truth from you than keep creating answers in my own head.

— Manti
